how we got here

From a road trip
to the corner store.

the spark

A creamy soda crush.

On a trip to Washington, Leylanee tried her first creamy soda, and couldn’t stop thinking about bringing that sweet-cream magic back to Long Beach.

the pop-up

Cremita Bonita Co ✕ Dream Drinks.

The collab started slinging creamy Red Bulls and dirty sodas as a pop-up. Cups first. The crowds followed.

the corner

An old pizza spot, reborn.

A tired storefront on 7th & Cherry got a second life, shelves went up, the cookers came in, and the windows filled with flavor.

grand opening · 2026

The lounge opens.

A whole wall of imported ramen, HAUSCOOK stations at the counter, and the soda bar in full swing. Long Beach showed up hungry.

today

The neighborhood hangout.

Board games on the shelf, watch parties on the screen, a trinket trading post by the door, and noodles until 10:30 every night.

the vibe

No kitchen. No waiter.
No rush.

You cook, you mix, you stay a while. That’s the whole idea.
