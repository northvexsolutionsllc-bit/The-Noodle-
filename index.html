our story

from a dirty-soda pop-up
to the neighborhood lounge.

Owner Leylanee Hernandez fell for creamy sodas on a trip to Washington, brought the idea home as a pop-up, and turned it into a Korean-convenience-store-inspired hangout on the corner of 7th & Cherry. Grab a packet, cook it yourself, stay for the vibe.

how it works

build your own bowl.

Four steps. Zero rules. Perfect noodles every single time.

  1. 01

    browse the wall

    Pick your base from a massive curation of imported Korean ramen — cheesy, creamy, or face-melting Buldak spicy.

  2. 02

    stack the top

    Cheese, soft-boiled egg, green onions, kimchi, rice cakes, shrimp — pile on your add-ons for a buck each.

  3. 03

    press start

    Set your bowl on our automated HAUSCOOK stations — hot water, timer, done. Cooked fresh, right at the counter.

  4. 04

    mix a drink

    Pair it with a custom creamy Red Bull, a refresher, or a signature dirty soda. Sweet heat, meet cream.
